About ASB International

A Nigerian private limited company headquartered in Tarauni, Kano State, serving public and private sector clients.

Overview

Company overview

ASB International & Gen. Services Limited was registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission on 23 May 2023. It was established to set up, manage and maintain telecommunication systems of every description, including telephony, telegraphy, radio and television, to install, supply and maintain the plant and equipment those systems rely on, and to provide general services including automotive general services.

Through our digital platforms, we sit where telecom infrastructure, technology services and general business solutions meet.
