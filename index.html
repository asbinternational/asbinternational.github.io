Who we are

A Nigerian company built around connectivity

Registered in May 2023 and headquartered in Tarauni, Kano State, ASB serves public and private sector clients. We were established to set up, manage and maintain telecommunication systems, to supply and maintain the equipment they rely on, and to provide general services including automotive support.

Through our digital platforms, we sit where telecom infrastructure, technology services and everyday business solutions meet.
